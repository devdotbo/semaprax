# Loan candidate inventory reuse v1

Status: source checkpoint; executable qualification pending.

The authenticated funded loan producer already checks the exact current
function pointer against its immutable program before lexical/candidate work.
It now constructs a private paid CurrentSubject at that check, without adding
another membership scan or inferring authority from stable IDs.

Candidate inspection retains its full discovery and complete alias/binding/
ownership inventory order. All five positive branches use one inline macro
that prepays the complete Inventory initializer and future affine Parts result/
receiving tuple before moving the actual maps. The complete candidate Result
and receiving Option are paid even on the negative route. Subject initializer,
receiving and reference-return controls are paid in full, with no prior-header
credit. Map backing stays paid cumulatively across CFG construction; no drop
refund, quota, exemption or inferred cached verdict is introduced.

The positive plan receiver consumes the opaque inventory exactly once. It checks
actual program, function and reservation pointers before touching any account.
Its complete consuming result was prepaid by the producer. A foreign subject or
same-budget foreign reservation receives an allocation-free inline diagnostic
without charging/poisoning either account. Matching ownership authenticates the
actual current CfgReservation with a zero request, preserving Detached and sticky
first refusal. The immutable maps then move into the unchanged CFG/plan builder;
no map data supplies CFG, plan or admission authority. For valid producer context,
CFG errors still precede all later plan semantics. No second inventory loop,
second map nodes or second place clones execute.

Old full candidate, plan builder and funded facade remain independently compiled.
Their complete old work exact/one-short assertions run against that prior facade.
The actual retained route has a separate exact/one-short/zero work receipt; work
for the eliminated loop is no longer performed. All original ceilings remain.
Full plan and six-field diagnostic equality, complete allocation/Drop, false
candidate, byte exact/one-short/zero/sticky and exact foreign-owner controls are
source-authored. Source joins include every new helper and whole prior receiver.

The normalizer's actual 64-requested/60-remaining receipt identifies only the
canonical preparation stage. This source checkpoint does not identify that inner
allocation or prove that this optimization alone recovers the application. New
control debits are real; no runtime, ABI-frame or whole-pipeline funding claim
follows from source layout sums or a source review.
