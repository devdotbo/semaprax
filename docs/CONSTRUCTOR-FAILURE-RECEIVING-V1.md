# Constructor failure receiving v1

Status: private source implementation of the first connected OPT825 receiving boundary. No compiler/runtime qualification, application-fit, full-constructor funding or issue-closure claim. This is not the selected replay report protocol.

## First connected boundary

The actual resolved-core attempt acquires one affine `constructor_failure::receiving::Receiver` from its already active builder ledger, immediately before synthetic module construction. It reserves the complete initialized receiving controls and actual one-Diagnostic Vec backing before acquisition; no new budget, allowance, floor, refund or old-header credit exists. Its empty capacity remains genuinely acquired and charged on successful builds and legacy-Many errors. Each retry acquires and pays its own owner under the unchanged per-attempt/max-debit protocol. New cost can cause a formerly fitting boundary to refuse; it is not hidden or normalized.

`Failure::One` moves the complete original Diagnostic into that held slot. The live identity check borrows the original captured ClassifierLedger; a foreign owner poisons the original and retains primary+unused backing inline. Detachment stays sticky after restoration. A correctly bound selected semantic Diagnostic can move after a sticky budget refusal without another reserve, Vec acquisition, formatting or replacement of any field. The original budget remains authoritative and the enclosing attempt still captures its first receipt. `Failure::Many` moves every existing element/order/backing and remains explicitly legacy: neither the Vec nor its payload becomes certified paid by this receiver.

The slot certifies only its actual receiving backing. Original Diagnostic message/path/help ownership remains with its original producer. In particular old helper-internal dynamic limit/help construction is not silently funded by the slot. A published owner retains the same ledger through restoration; moving the held Vec at compatibility output does not re-enter or reset it.

Setup refusal remains typed/inline through the actual attempt result. Only Budget maps to the original builder-byte diagnostic using the captured maximum. NoOwner, Allocation, Capacity and Arithmetic are distinct internal setup refusals, never invented byte receipts. The compatibility adapter materializes their report explicitly outside the funded receiving boundary. The full primary survives foreign association; a secondary authority error never replaces it.

## Complete independent prior receiver

The entire previous `build_resolved_core_current` body is compiled in a test-only child, including its original One-to-Vec conversion and every helper/order/error. Its only lexical adapter is function visibility plus explicit imports. Complete old defaults/stubs/overlay/Failure remain unchanged. Source controls compare the whole original body, full linked HIR and generated C; builder debit metadata deliberately changes and is never normalized to force equality.

Authored receiving controls cover exact/one-short/zero setup, actual slot pointer/capacity, all six static diagnostic fields, prior sticky first receipt, foreign/restored detachment, complete Many order/dynamic fields/JSON, unused-slot Drop, and whole construct-to-Drop physical backing. They are unexecuted until the owning grouped build/run records results.
